Takers and order come from FPL. Pen share is the share of the team's penalties a player is expected to take next gameweek: the number 1 takes 95% of those awarded while he is on the pitch, and each next taker gets the ones left while those above him are off, using Adjusted Min. For example, a number 1 on for 60 minutes gets 63%, and a number 2 who plays all 90 gets 32%. xPen goals is his expected penalty goals in the match. Penalties per match is the team's expected penalties, from the league rate and the team's attack.
