Minutes appear here once the GitHub update has run.

Adjust min sets one figure for every upcoming gameweek; injuries and bans still apply on top of it (a gameweek he is out stays 0). Type in a gameweek box to set just that one, which also overrides an injury or ban. Clear a box to go back to the model. Your minutes are saved in this browser and used on the xPoints page. Double gameweeks show minutes per match, for example 0+84.

Played gameweeks show the minutes he actually played; crossed-out minutes are matches he missed through injury, illness or a ban (from premierinjuries.com absence history, plus the match he went off injured in), which are left out of xMin. Usual is the last 5 matches that count, weighted 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 from newest to oldest; one-off absences of a regular starter and red-card bans are skipped, and 2 to 5 straight matches of 70+ after a clearly lower one count on their own.

Injuries and bans come from the premierinjuries.com injury table first, then FPL news: 0 until the potential return date, then the chance given (for example 75%) or full. No return date: 0 in every gameweek. A red card in his last match not yet listed counts as a 1-match ban. Doubtful players on FPL only: that chance in the next gameweek, back to full over the following 4.

Easing back: after an injury a player gets his minutes from before it, times a share for each of his first matches back. The share depends on how long the injury was (short, 2-4 weeks, 4+ weeks) and is learned from past returns; players injured 3 or more times in the last 12 months get the slower injury-prone curve.
