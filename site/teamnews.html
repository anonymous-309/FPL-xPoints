    News, flags and chances of playing come straight from the official FPL site, checked each time this site updates. Out is a player FPL marks injured or unavailable, Doubts one it marks doubtful (with FPL's chance of playing the next round), Banned a suspended player, and Other news a note on a player who is available. Likely XI is not FPL's: it starts from the club's line-up in its last league match (from FotMob), so every player stands where he actually played (striker, winger, central midfield, full-back…). A starter we expect fewer than 45 minutes from next (injured, banned, doubtful or rotated, by our minutes formula) is swapped for the player of the same FPL position with the most expected minutes, and a player back from injury (or anyone else outside the XI) takes the place of the starter of his FPL position we expect the fewest minutes from, when we expect him to play at least 10 minutes more; a blue ring marks him, and the line under the pitch says who came in for whom. The number under each name is his expected minutes. One booking from a ban lists players a yellow card short of the next suspension limit. Times are GMT. Not affiliated with the Premier League.
