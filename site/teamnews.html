    News, flags and chances of playing come straight from the official FPL site, checked each time this site updates. Out is a player FPL marks injured or unavailable, Doubts one it marks doubtful (with FPL's chance of playing the next round), Banned a suspended player, and Other news a note on a player who is available. Likely XI is not FPL's: it keeps the formation and the places of the club's last league line-up (from FotMob) and fills every place with the players who fit it best, judged on two things together: how many minutes we expect from each player next gameweek, and how well he suits that place by FotMob's list of his positions (his main one first: right back, centre back, left back, holding or central midfield, wing, striker…). So when a centre back is out, the right back can move inside and another right back come in, if that adds up to more. A blue ring marks a player who was not in the last league XI; the line under the pitch lists who is in and who is out. The number under each name is his expected minutes. One booking from a ban lists players a yellow card short of the next suspension limit. Times are GMT. Not affiliated with the Premier League.
