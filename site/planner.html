Enter an FPL team ID

Where do I find my ID?
  1. Open fantasy.premierleague.com in a browser and sign in.
  2. Go to the Points tab.
  3. Look at the address bar: it reads fantasy.premierleague.com/entry/1234567/event/11. The number after /entry/ is your team ID.

The FPL phone app does not show it, so use a browser (on your phone is fine). The ID is public and only lets the planner read your team; it cannot change anything.

This gameweek in detail

Force in / Force out (Settings): Force a transfer in this gameweek, for one you have already made or want made. It counts against your free transfers like any other; the plan picks the rest. Cleared when the gameweek moves on.

Plans to look at (Settings): Plans to look at, by the free transfers you have: for saving now (each with different moves the next gameweek), for using 1, 2, … now, and with a hit. Up to 10 in all; more plans take longer to work out.

Plan my own transfers: Your squad on the pitch as in a plan (starting 11, captain and bench picked for you), one tab per transfer gameweek. Tap a player for his xPoints row, then Replace him (pick who comes in: same position, affordable, with the gain that gameweek and to the last gameweek) or Remove him (or take back a player you bought that week); tap any gameweek in his row to see its figures. Replace Player lists who can come in with the gain that gameweek and to the last; the line-up updates at once. Then press Work out my plan: the planner picks the best starting 11, captain and bench every week, counts free transfers and hits, and scores it like the automatic plans; no other transfers are made.

Suggest transfers works out plans for each number of free transfers you could use in the coming gameweek: saving them (in several branches, each with different moves the gameweek after), using 1, 2, … up to all of them, and with a hit; how many of each is set in Settings, 10 at most. Each plan then makes its own best moves for the next 4 gameweeks (transfers are planned for 5 gameweeks in all), and after that the squad stays to the end of the gameweeks you plan, apart from a Wildcard or Free Hit you set. Plan my own transfers takes your own transfers, gameweek by gameweek, and scores them the same way.

Score = expected points (xPoints figures, with your minutes edits from the Minutes page; with Decay each later gameweek counting 0.85 times the one before, with Normal all the same) + FT bonus: in every transfer gameweek each free transfer saved is worth 0.5 points (4 at most, so 2 points), weighed like its gameweek, and those still saved after the 5th gameweek earn it again in every gameweek to the end. Show & optimise decides how plans are worked out and which is Best: Decay weighs each later gameweek 0.85 times the one before (nearer points count more, as they are surer), Normal counts every gameweek the same. The figures in the results are shown the same way. The plan with the highest score is marked Best. Rule: a gameweek's moves must gain at least 0.5 that same gameweek for each free transfer they use (not the 5th one when you hold 5, which cannot be saved; a gameweek with a hit is exempt, since a hit pays back over the weeks after). Because prices can change, a transfer planned n gameweeks ahead must leave at least 0.1m × n in the bank; plans that leave only that little show their ITB in orange.

Squad rules: 15 players (2 GK, 5 DEF, 5 MID, 3 FWD), at most 3 per club, within your bank and selling prices; one free transfer a gameweek, up to 5 saved, 4 points for every extra one; a Wildcard or Free Hit keeps your saved transfers. Points include auto-subs: a starter who does not play is replaced by the first bench player who plays and keeps a legal formation, and the vice-captain takes the armband if the captain does not play. The last column of the plans is the share of 5,000 simulated runs of every match in which a plan has the highest score.

Tap a player on the pitch for his figures that gameweek (as on the xPoints page) and his long-term expected points over the gameweeks you plan (weighed 0.85 a week); Replace opens My own transfers with this plan's moves and him going out, and Remove (for a player the plan buys) takes that transfer back and plans again. Your team is read from FPL when you make a plan, through the site's small proxy. Plans are worked out on your device, several at once on phones and computers with more cores.
