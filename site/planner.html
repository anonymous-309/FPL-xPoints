Settings

Force a transfer in this gameweek, for one you have already made or want made. It counts against your free transfers like any other; the plan picks the rest. Cleared when the gameweek moves on.

For the coming gameweek the planner works out plans for each number of free transfers you could use: with 1 or 2 free transfers the best 3 plans for each number, with 3 the best 2, with 4 or 5 the best 2 for using 2 or 3 and the best 1 otherwise, and saving the transfer. Each plan then makes the best moves for the next 2 gameweeks (transfers are planned for 3 gameweeks in all), and after that the squad stays, apart from a Wildcard or Free Hit you set. Plans are compared on expected points from the xPoints figures (with your own minutes edits from the Minutes page, if any), with each later gameweek counting 0.85 times the one before; free transfers or money kept are not given a value for now. Rules: a squad of 15 (2 GK, 5 DEF, 5 MID, 3 FWD), at most 3 per club, within your bank and selling prices; one free transfer a gameweek, up to 5 saved, 4 points for every extra one; a Wildcard or Free Hit keeps your saved transfers. Points include auto-subs: a starter who does not play is replaced by the first bench player who plays and keeps a legal formation, and the vice-captain takes the armband if the captain does not play; while planning, bench players count by the chance they are needed, from the appearance chances of your likely starters. "Best" is the share of 5,000 simulated runs of every match in which a plan scores the most. Prices are assumed not to change. Your team is read from FPL when you make a plan, through the site's small proxy. Not affiliated with the Premier League.
