Planner

Enter an FPL team ID

The plan picks the transfers, chips, starting 11 and captain that give the most expected points over the gameweeks you choose, using the xPoints figures (with your own minutes edits from the Minutes page, if any). Transfers are planned for the first 3 gameweeks only, since things change fast; after that the squad stays, apart from a Wildcard or Free Hit you set. Rules: a squad of 15 (2 GK, 5 DEF, 5 MID, 3 FWD), at most 3 per club, within your bank and selling prices; one free transfer a gameweek, up to 5 saved, 4 points for every extra one; a Wildcard or Free Hit keeps your saved transfers. Points shown include auto-subs: a starter who does not play is replaced by the first bench player who plays and keeps a legal formation, and the vice-captain takes the armband if the captain does not play. Prices are assumed not to change. Your team (squad, bank, free transfers, chips, transfers already made) is read from FPL when you make a plan, through the site's small proxy; if FPL cannot be reached, the copy saved at the last site update is used for the team IDs in planner_ids.txt. Not affiliated with the Premier League.
