Where do I find my ID?
  1. Open fantasy.premierleague.com in a browser and sign in.
  2. Go to the Points tab.
  3. Look at the address bar: it reads fantasy.premierleague.com/entry/1234567/event/11. The number after /entry/ is your team ID.

The FPL phone app does not show it, so use a browser (on your phone is fine). The ID is public and only lets the planner read your team; it cannot change anything.

This gameweek in detail

Show & optimise sets how the gameweeks are weighed.

Decay: each later gameweek counts 0.85 times the one before (GW+1 × 0.85, GW+2 × 0.72, …), since nearer points are surer.

Normal: every gameweek counts the same.

The plans are worked out, the Best plan is picked and all figures are shown this way (labelled xPts Decay or xPts).

Keep: the player is in the squad in every plan, every gameweek.

Avoid: the player is never bought in any plan (one you own is sold if a plan wants).

Pick a name from the list; it is added straight away. Tap × to take it off.

Your squad on the pitch as in a plan (starting 11, captain and bench picked for you), one tab per transfer gameweek. Tap a player for his xPoints row, then Replace him (pick who comes in: same position, affordable, with the gain that gameweek and to the last gameweek) or Remove him (or take back a player you bought that week); tap any gameweek in his row to see its figures. Replace Player lists who can come in with the gain that gameweek and to the last; the line-up updates at once. Your moves are worked out as a plan straight away (shown below, with the best Free Hit week): the planner picks the best starting 11, captain and bench every week, counts free transfers and hits, and scores it like the automatic plans; no other transfers are made.

Suggest transfers works out plans for each number of free transfers you could use in the coming gameweek: saving them (in several branches, each with different moves the gameweek after), using 1, 2, … up to all of them, and with a hit; how many of each is set in Settings, 10 at most. Each plan then makes its own best moves for the next 4 gameweeks (transfers are planned for 5 gameweeks in all), and after that the squad stays to the end of the gameweeks you plan, apart from a Wildcard or Free Hit you set. Plan my own transfers takes your own transfers, gameweek by gameweek, and scores them the same way.

Score = expected points (xPoints figures, with your minutes edits from the Minutes page; with Decay each later gameweek counting 0.85 times the one before, with Normal all the same) + FT bonus: in every transfer gameweek each free transfer saved is worth 0.5 points (4 at most, so 2 points), weighed like its gameweek, and those still saved after the 5th gameweek earn it again in every gameweek to the end. Show & optimise decides how plans are worked out and which is Best: Decay weighs each later gameweek 0.85 times the one before (nearer points count more, as they are surer), Normal counts every gameweek the same. The figures in the results are shown the same way. The plan with the highest score is marked Best. Rule: a gameweek's moves must gain at least 0.5 that same gameweek for each free transfer they use (not the 5th one when you hold 5, which cannot be saved; a gameweek with a hit is exempt, since a hit pays back over the weeks after). Because prices can change, a transfer planned n gameweeks ahead must leave at least 0.1m × n in the bank; plans that leave only that little show their Budget saved in orange.

Squad rules: 15 players (2 GK, 5 DEF, 5 MID, 3 FWD), at most 3 per club, within your bank and selling prices; one free transfer a gameweek, up to 5 saved, 4 points for every extra one; a Wildcard or Free Hit keeps your saved transfers. Points include auto-subs: a starter who does not play is replaced by the first bench player who plays and keeps a legal formation, and the vice-captain takes the armband if the captain does not play. The last column of the plans is the share of 5,000 simulated runs of every match in which a plan has the highest score.

Tap a player on the pitch for his figures that gameweek (as on the xPoints page) and his long-term expected points over the gameweeks you plan (weighed 0.85 a week); Replace opens My own transfers with this plan's moves and him going out, and Remove (for a player the plan buys) takes that transfer back and plans again. Your team is read from FPL when you make a plan, through the site's small proxy. Plans are worked out on your device, several at once on phones and computers with more cores.

Uses now: how many free transfers the plan uses in the first gameweek: Save (none), 1 FT, 2 FT, … or with a hit. #2, #3 are the next best plans of the same kind; the Save ones differ in their moves the gameweek after.

Each gameweek column: that gameweek's transfers (out → in), or Save. Under them:

FT saved: free transfers left after that gameweek's transfers.

Budget saved: money left in the bank after them. In orange when it leaves no more than 0.1m × weeks ahead, so a price change could stop the move.

+x GWn: what those moves gain in that gameweek (hits taken off).

+x to the last GW: what they gain from that gameweek to the end of the plan against the squad before them (with Decay, weighed 0.85 a week).

xPts: the plan's expected points over all the gameweeks planned, auto-subs, captain and chips included, hits taken off.

Bench: from the real teams of 1000 top managers, a bench player comes in (auto-sub, or moved into the 11 for a starter who is out) in 28% of gameweeks for sub 1, 12% for sub 2, 5% for sub 3 and 3% for the reserve keeper. So the bench counts at those shares, and the starters count a little less: the keeper 97%, each outfield starter 95.5%.

With Decay each later gameweek counts 0.85 times the one before (xPts Decay); with Normal all count the same.

Gain: the plan's xPts minus what your squad scores if you make no transfers (same captain picking and auto-subs). It is the sum of the gains under each gameweek's moves.

FT bonus: credit for free transfers kept for later. In every transfer gameweek each free transfer saved is worth 0.5 (4 at most, so 2 points), weighed like its gameweek; those still saved at the end of the transfer weeks earn it again in every gameweek to the end.

Score = xPts + FT bonus. The plan with the highest Score is the Best plan, highlighted in the table.

Best: every match is simulated 5,000 times; this is the share of those runs in which the plan has the highest Score of all the plans in the table (they add up to 100%).

A plan can have the best expected Score and still come top less often, when its points come from rarer big hauls.

Force a transfer in this gameweek, for one you have already made or want made. It counts against your free transfers like any other; the plan picks the rest. Cleared when the gameweek moves on.

Plans to look at, by the free transfers you have: for saving now (each with different moves the next gameweek), for using 1, 2, … now, and with a hit. Up to 10 in all; more plans take longer to work out.
