Advanced

The planner finds the best 3 transfer options for the first gameweek, 2 for the second and 1 for the third, with saving the transfer as a choice at every step, and one path after that; each picks the transfers, starting 11 and captain that give the most expected points over the gameweeks you choose (later gameweeks count a little less, by the decay setting), using the xPoints figures (with your own minutes edits from the Minutes page, if any). Transfers are planned for the first 3 gameweeks by default (Settings), since things change fast; after that the squad stays, apart from a Wildcard or Free Hit you set. Rules: a squad of 15 (2 GK, 5 DEF, 5 MID, 3 FWD), at most 3 per club, within your bank and selling prices; one free transfer a gameweek, up to 5 saved, 4 points for every extra one; a Wildcard or Free Hit keeps your saved transfers. Free transfers kept are worth points, each one less than the one before (Settings: 3, 2.5, 1.5, 0.5, 0), and a transfer must add at least 1.5 xPts, except selling a player injured, banned or unavailable for the next 3 gameweeks. Bench players count by the chance they are needed, from how many of your likely starters may not play. The recommended plan has the best score, unless it beats the best plan with fewer transfers in under 60% of the simulated runs; then the safer plan is recommended. Points shown include auto-subs: a starter who does not play is replaced by the first bench player who plays and keeps a legal formation, and the vice-captain takes the armband if the captain does not play. Prices are assumed not to change. Your team (squad, bank, free transfers, chips, transfers already made) is read from FPL when you make a plan, through the site's small proxy; if FPL cannot be reached, the copy saved at the last site update is used for the team IDs in planner_ids.txt.
