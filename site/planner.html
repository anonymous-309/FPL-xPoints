Settings

Force a transfer in this gameweek, for one you have already made or want made. It counts against your free transfers like any other; the plan picks the rest. Cleared when the gameweek moves on.

The planner decides one gameweek at a time, for 3 gameweeks. For the squad as it stands it finds the best 5 choices (saving the transfer, or using 1, 2, … free transfers, or one hit), judging each by its expected points from that gameweek to the end of the plan if the squad then stayed, plus 1.5 points for each free transfer carried into the next gameweek, so a transfer is only made when it gains more than that. Each GW1 choice is followed up the same way in GW2 and GW3; after each step the best path from every GW1 choice is kept, and the best others up to 8 paths at the end. After the transfer weeks the squad stays, apart from a Wildcard or Free Hit you set. Expected points come from the xPoints figures (with your own minutes edits from the Minutes page, if any); each later gameweek counts 0.85 times the one before. Rules: a squad of 15 (2 GK, 5 DEF, 5 MID, 3 FWD), at most 3 per club, within your bank and selling prices; one free transfer a gameweek, up to 5 saved, 4 points for every extra one; a Wildcard or Free Hit keeps your saved transfers. Points include auto-subs: a starter who does not play is replaced by the first bench player who plays and keeps a legal formation, and the vice-captain takes the armband if the captain does not play; while planning, bench players count by the chance they are needed, from the appearance chances of your likely starters. "Best" is the share of 5,000 simulated runs of every match in which a plan scores the most. Prices are assumed not to change. Your team is read from FPL when you make a plan, through the site's small proxy. Not affiliated with the Premier League.
