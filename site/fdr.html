Fixture Difficulty Rating (FDR) for each club's coming matches, 1 (easiest) to 5 (hardest), in the same colours as the official FPL site. The ratings are our own, not FPL's, and are for reference only: the planner and the points do not use them. They come from the same model as the points. Attack rates the goals the club is expected to score in the match (the other side's expected goals conceded); more is easier. Defence rates the club's chance of a clean sheet; higher is easier. Overall rates expected goals scored minus conceded. Each match gets 1 to 5 by where it falls among all the coming matches shown, a fifth of them on each rating; hover or tap a cell for the numbers behind it. Avg FDR is the average over the club's matches in the gameweeks chosen. Clubs are sorted easiest first by the model's total over those gameweeks: a blank gameweek adds nothing, a double adds both matches. (H) is at home, (A) away. Not affiliated with the Premier League.
