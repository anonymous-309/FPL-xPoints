For reference only: the planner and the points do not use these ratings. They come from the same model as the points. Attack: goals the club is expected to score in the match (the other side's expected goals conceded), shown in each cell; more is easier. Defence: the club's chance of a clean sheet, shown in each cell; higher is easier. Overall: expected goals scored minus conceded, shown in each cell. Each cell is coloured 1 (easiest) to 5 (hardest) by where it falls among all the coming matches shown, a fifth of them in each colour. Clubs are sorted easiest first by the total over the gameweeks chosen: a blank gameweek adds nothing, a double adds both matches. Opponents in capitals are at home, in lower case away. Not affiliated with the Premier League.
