How it works

The Chance 90min figures and how xPoints is calculated

The idea

A player's xPoints for a gameweek comes from two steps:

  1. If he plays the full 90 minutes, how many points he is expected to get from each part of the FPL scoring (goals, assists, clean sheet, bonus and so on). This is what the Chance 90min page shows.
  2. Scale by his expected minutes (xMin, on the Minutes page). Parts that build up with time on the pitch are multiplied by xMin/90. Clean sheet and goals conceded only count with 60+ minutes, so they are multiplied by his chance of playing 60+ minutes.

xPoints = appearance points + Σ (points from each part over 90 minutes × minutes factor)

In a double gameweek each match is worked out on its own and the two are added up.

Step 1: minutes (xMin)

  • The 5-4-3-2-1 formula: take his last 5 matches, weight the latest by 5, the one before by 4, then 3, 2 and 1, and divide by 15. For example, latest first 90, 90, 60, 90, 0 gives xMin = (90×5 + 90×4 + 60×3 + 90×2 + 0×1) / 15 = 78.
  • Matches he missed for reasons other than selection are skipped: yellow card bans, injuries (from the Injuries log and any the admin added) and the first match after a Doubtful flag. Older matches fill the gap so there are always 5.
  • Red cards: he misses the ban, then goes back to 100% of the xMin he had before the card.
  • FPL flags: a 75% flag multiplies next gameweek's xMin by 75%. Doubtful only affects the next match. An injury with a return date gives 0 until that gameweek; one without a return date gives 0 for every gameweek.
  • Admin min: a figure the admin sets on the Admin page replaces the formula for everyone. On the Minutes page, any viewer can also set their own xMin; those edits stay in their browser only.
  • Appearance 1+ and 60+: the same 5-4-3-2-1 weights over the share of matches he played at all and the share where he played 60+ minutes, multiplied by the FPL flag.

Step 2: the Chance 90min columns

Every column except the two Appearance ones assumes he plays the full 90 minutes. A column shown as a percentage is the chance of it happening at least once; the others are expected numbers (averages). The two are linked: chance of at least one = 1 − e−expected number. For example, 0.70 expected goals in total gives 1 − e−0.70 ≈ 50% to score at least once.

Every rate blends two things: the player's own record (recent matches count more; a match's weight halves every 12 gameweeks) and the average of players in the same role. The fewer minutes he has, the more he is pulled towards that average, so one lucky match does not inflate his figures.

The full xPoints formula

For one match, with m = xMin/90, P1 = Appearance 1+ and P60 = Appearance 60+:

Cards−(expected yellows + 3 × expected reds) × m

When you edit xMin on the Minutes or xPoints page, the site recalculates with this formula at the new minutes. Appearance points then follow FPL rules: 1 point if the minutes are above 0, 1 more at 60 or above.
