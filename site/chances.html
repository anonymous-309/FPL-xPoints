Opponents in capitals are home matches, lower case are away. Appearance 1+ and 60+ are the chance he plays at least 1 or 60 minutes, from his recent matches and FPL flags. Every other column assumes he plays the full 90 minutes of each match. Percentages are the chance of at least one; assists follow FPL rules. xGoals, xAssists, Conceded, Saves and Bonus are expected numbers. Clean sheet and Conceded are for the team. Saves and Pen save are for goalkeepers only. A double gameweek covers both matches.
