Opponents in capitals are home matches, lower case are away. Start is the chance he starts: the share of his last 5 matches he started, weighted 5-4-3-2-1 (newest first, the same matches as his usual minutes), times the share of his usual minutes he is expected to play now (FPL flags, your edits). Appearance 1+ and 60+ are the chance he plays at least 1 or 60 minutes, from his recent matches and FPL flags; xPoints uses them for appearance points. Every other column assumes he plays the full 90 minutes of each match. Percentages are the chance of at least one; assists follow FPL rules. NPxGoals is open-play goals (no penalties), from his non-penalty goals, non-penalty xG and non-penalty xG on target; Pen goals is expected goals from penalties, from his place on the penalty list and when the takers above him are on the pitch (your edits on the Minutes page count): a listed taker with a Start above 50% is on from the kick-off for his minutes, one below comes on for them at the end, and the first listed taker on the pitch takes the penalty. Two takers above him who both started leave him only the end of the match, after they have gone off. Penalties while no listed taker is on go to the free kick and corner takers, split by their minutes, so a team's shares add up to 100%. Score 1+ includes both. NPxGoals, Pen goals, xAssists, Conceded, Saves and Bonus are expected numbers. Clean sheet and Conceded are for the team. Saves and Pen save are for goalkeepers only. DefCon actions is the expected number of defensive actions FPL counts (clearances, blocks, interceptions, tackles, and recoveries for midfielders and forwards); DefCon is the chance of reaching 10 (defenders) or 12. BPS is the expected bonus points system score; bonus goes to the top 3 BPS in each match, so Bonus also weighs him against the best players in the same fixture. A double gameweek covers both matches.
