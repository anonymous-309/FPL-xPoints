Opponents in capitals are home matches, lower case are away. Expected minutes for every gameweek, including injuries and bans, are on the Minutes page; minutes you set there are used here, and those cells are outlined. Avg xMin is the average expected minutes per match over the gameweeks shown (3, 5 or 8; blanks left out); it can only be changed on the Minutes page. Hover over or select a cell to see the minutes, Chance 90min (the same figures as the Chance 90min page) and the points from each component. Appearance points follow FPL rules: 1 point for 1+ minutes and another for 60+. Clean sheet and goals conceded need 60+ minutes, so they are scaled by the chance he plays 60+; every other component is scaled by minutes/90. These are statistical estimates, for reference only.
