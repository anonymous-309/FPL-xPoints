Opponents in capitals are home matches, lower case are away. xMin is expected minutes from recent matches, with newer matches weighted more; it skips one-off absences and red-card bans, and spots players returning from injury. Adjusted Min is xMin reduced when FPL flags an injury or suspension. Select a cell to see where the points come from. Edit Adjusted Min to recalculate with your own minutes for every gameweek, or select a gameweek cell to change just that one; clear the box to go back to the model figure. Appearance points follow FPL rules: 1 point for 1+ minutes and another for 60+. Every other component, including clean sheets, is scaled by Adjusted Min/90. These are statistical estimates, for reference only. Not affiliated with the Premier League.
