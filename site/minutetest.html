Last season, tested as if live: before every gameweek the model was run with only what was known then, and its xMin (expected minutes, chance of playing included) is set against the minutes he really played. Matches where he was known to be out (listed injured or suspended, or not in the matchday squad) count as xMin 0 and are marked out. Diff: average xMin less average minutes (above 0 = the model expected more). Miss: the average gap, either way. Tap a player for each match. Not affiliated with the Premier League.
