How the test works: the model is trained only on matches before a gameweek, predicts the next 4 gameweeks, then moves on, through a whole past season it never saw. Injuries and absences of past seasons come from FotMob's lists of unavailable players and Soccerway's injury records, so the test knows who was out before each match, as the site does from FPL's flags. Its numbers are then set against what really happened, next to two simple ways of guessing: each player's points in his last 4 gameweeks, and his points per game over a long run. The test reruns every time the site updates. Full output (text). Not affiliated with the Premier League.
