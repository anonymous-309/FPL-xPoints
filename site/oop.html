A player is out of position (OOP) when FPL lists him as a defender but he plays further forward. Two ways show it. His recent touches in the opposition box and non-penalty xG + xA per 90 are both at or above the median midfielder (recent: his last few matches, newer ones weighted more, at least about 270 minutes): he counts as a midfielder. Or FotMob's line-ups show him starting on a wing, in wide midfield or as an attacking midfielder in some of his last 5 league starts (holding or central midfield and wing-back do not count): he counts as a midfielder that share of the time (the newest start weighted 5, then 4, 3, 2, 1; shown from 25%), so his goal, assist and bonus chances are a mix of the defender and midfielder figures by that share. Last starts lists his place in each of those starts, oldest first (RW right wing, LW left wing, AM attacking midfield, RCM/LCM, RDM/LDM, RB, LB, RCB/LCB…). For OOP players the model predicts goals, assists and bonus from the role he plays, and still scores the points by his FPL position, so an OOP defender keeps 6 points a goal and 4 for a clean sheet.
